Chocolate Chip Cookies

Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 12 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cup flour
  • 1 cup butter
  • 3/4 cupbrown suger
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

Mix all ingredients togather. Drop spoonfuls on baking sheet. Bake at 350°F for 12 minutes or until golden brown.

Difficulty: Easy
